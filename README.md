VietinBank NDC Overview BI Report** is an interactive Power BI report designed to provide an overview and detailed analysis of VietinBank’s deposit and lending performance.

The report consolidates key banking performance indicators into a centralized dashboard, allowing users to monitor balances, customer activity, products, channels, maturity profiles, and changes over time. It supports both high-level management monitoring and detailed customer-level analysis through interactive filters and drill-through pages.

# Main Objectives

The report is designed to:

* Monitor the overall performance of **deposit and lending activities**.
* Track changes in deposit balances and loan outstanding balances compared with previous periods.
* Analyze customer segments, banking products, business units, relationship managers, and transaction channels.
* Identify customers with significant increases or decreases in deposits and loan balances.
* Monitor upcoming deposit maturities and loan disbursements.
* Provide detailed customer and account-level information for further investigation.
* Support management and business teams in monitoring performance and identifying business opportunities.

# Report Structure

The report consists of eight main pages:

1. **Deposit Overview**
   Provides a high-level view of deposit performance, including total deposit balance, customer count, number of deposit products, average balance, balance changes, deposit composition by account type, and opening channel. The page also highlights the Top 5 customers with the largest increases and decreases in deposit balances.

2. **Deposit Change Analysis**
   Enables users to compare deposit balances between two selected reporting dates. The analysis focuses on customer-level changes and provides a clear view of increases and decreases in deposit balances.

3. **Deposit Maturity**
   Provides information about upcoming deposit maturities, including maturity dates, products, terms, interest rates, balances, and the number of days until maturity. Users can analyze upcoming maturities by different time periods and customer groups.

4. **Deposit Detail**
   Provides detailed information for individual deposit customers and accounts. Users can review deposit products, account types, relationship managers, channels, terms, interest rates, opening dates, maturity dates, and balance-related metrics.

5. **Loan Overview**
   Provides a high-level overview of lending performance, including total outstanding loan balance, customer count, number of loan products, average balance, balance changes, loan portfolio composition, and loan classification. The page also identifies the Top 5 customers with the largest increases and decreases in outstanding loan balances.

6. **Loan Maturity / Disbursement**
   Provides an overview of upcoming loan-related activities, including expected disbursements, loan products, loan classifications, terms, balances, and upcoming dates. This page supports monitoring of the loan portfolio and future business activities.

7. **Loan Detail**
   Provides detailed information about individual loan accounts and customers, including loan products, business units, relationship managers, loan classifications, terms, preferential periods, interest rates, disbursement information, and outstanding balances.

8. **Customer Detail**
   Provides a consolidated customer-level view of both deposit and lending activities. Users can analyze historical balances, average balances, deposit and loan products, transaction activity, and other customer-related performance indicators.

### Key Interactive Features

The report includes several interactive features to support analysis:

* **Date filters** for selecting the reporting period.
* **Business unit / branch filters** for analyzing performance by organizational unit.
* **Relationship manager filters** for monitoring individual portfolio performance.
* **Customer filters** for customer-level analysis.
* **Dynamic comparison parameters** for comparing current performance with selected reference periods.
* **Drill-through functionality** to navigate from summary information to detailed customer, account, or business-unit views.
* **Interactive charts and tables** that dynamically respond to user selections.
